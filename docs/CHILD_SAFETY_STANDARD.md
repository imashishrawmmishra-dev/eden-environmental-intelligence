# EDEN Child Safety & Educational Protection Standard
**Compliance:** COPPA (15 U.S.C. §§ 6501–6506), FERPA (34 CFR Part 99), UK Age Appropriate Design Code

## 1. Zero Direct Minor Identifiers
EDEN does not require, prompt for, or store any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as student real names, home addresses, phone numbers, or social media handles. Student profiles in `data/eden_v5.db` use randomly generated pseudonymous UUIDs (`usr_xxxx`).

## 2. Curated Scientific Sandbox
- All knowledge nodes are restricted to established, peer-reviewed environmental science and accredited curriculum standards (APES, IB Environmental Systems, FE Environmental).
- Queries prompting the generation of harmful, weaponized chemical formulas (e.g. chlorine gas synthesis, chemical warfare agents) or hazardous waste dumping methods are programmatically intercepted and rejected.

## 3. Commercial Ad-Free Guarantee
EDEN contains zero third-party advertising networks, affiliate tracking beacons, or behavioural profiling SDKs.

## 4. School District Data Retention
Student progress records can be fully exported or expunged on request by school administrators, satisfying FERPA requirements.
