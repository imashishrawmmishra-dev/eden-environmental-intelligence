# EDEN Privacy Policy & Terms Template
**Last Updated:** September 2026

## 1. Information We Process
EDEN processes environmental questions submitted by users solely to provide answers and suggest pedagogical next steps. In offline mode, all processing occurs locally within the software instance. In optional Live AI mode, questions are processed via secure server-side API calls.

## 2. No Sale of User Data
We do not sell, rent, or trade user data or learning histories to any third party or marketing broker.

## 3. Educational Compliance
EDEN complies with the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). No biometric, geo-tracking, or advertising identifiers are collected.

## 4. Contact & Data Deletion
Users may purge their competency records and learning events at any time via the platform settings or by emailing support@eden-environmental.org.
