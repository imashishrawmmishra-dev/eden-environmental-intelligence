## Description
EDEN (Explore, Discover, Educate, Nurture) is an environmental intelligence and learning platform. Built around a comprehensive knowledge graph of environmental concepts (atmosphere, biodiversity, water systems, pollution indicators like BOD/DO, environmental law, and careers), EDEN allows students, researchers, and practitioners to:

- Ask complex questions and receive structured pedagogical actions (Learn, Connect, Practice, Simulate, Act)
- Explore mathematical simulations including the Streeter-Phelps river oxygen sag, global carbon budgets, and island biogeography
- Access curated authoritative external sources from IPCC, WMO, EPA, and IUCN
- Track competency mastery over time across key environmental disciplines
- Operates 100% offline with embedded knowledge reasoning
